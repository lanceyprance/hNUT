## Fat-soluble (DEKA)

| | Active form | Main job | Deficiency | Toxicity | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** retinol | retinol, retinal, retinoic acid (provitamin: β-carotene) | **vision** (rhodopsin), **gene expression** (RAR/RXR), immunity, reproduction | **night blindness → xerophthalmia** (Bitot's spots) → blindness; hyperkeratosis | liver + bone damage; **teratogenic** | liver, sweet potato, carrots, spinach |
| **K** | K1 phylloquinone, K2 menaquinone, K3 menadiol | **carboxylates glutamate → Gla** (binds Ca): **clotting**, bone | **VKDB** in newborns | none (no UL); **affects warfarin** | kale, spinach, broccoli, **gut bacteria** |
| **D** calciferol | **1,25(OH)₂D** (calcitriol); status = **25(OH)D** | **calcium homeostasis** (gut, kidney, bone); immune, β-cell | **rickets** (kids), **osteomalacia** (adults) | **high blood calcium** (not from sun) | **sun (UVB)**, oily fish, fortified foods |
| **E** tocopherol | **α-tocopherol** | **antioxidant** in membranes (protects PUFA); recharged by **vitamin C** | neurological, **haemolytic anaemia** | bleeding (blocks vit K) | **nuts, seeds, vegetable oils** |

## Water-soluble B vitamins

| | Coenzyme | Main job | Deficiency | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** thiamin | **TDP / TPP** | **oxidative decarboxylation** (pyruvate → acetyl-CoA); transketolase; nerves | **beriberi** (dry = nerves, wet = heart); **Wernicke-Korsakoff** (alcoholics) | **pork**, wholegrains, fortified cereal |
| **B2** riboflavin | **FAD, FMN** | **electron carrier** (FAD → FADH₂): ETC, β-oxidation, TCA; glutathione reductase | **glossitis, cheilosis, stomatitis** | **dairy**, eggs, almonds, meat |
| **B3** niacin | **NAD, NADP** | NAD⁺ = electron acceptor (energy); **NADPH** = biosynthesis (fatty acids); DNA repair | **pellagra: 4 Ds** | meat, poultry, fish; **tryptophan** (÷60) |
| **B5** pantothenic acid | **coenzyme A** | **acyl carrier** → acetyl-CoA; fatty acid, cholesterol, steroid synthesis | **burning feet**, fatigue (rare) | everywhere (*pantos*) |
| **B6** pyridoxine | **PLP** | **transamination**, decarboxylation (→ GABA); glycogen phosphorylase; steroid hormones | mood, skin, mild anaemia (rare) | chickpeas, potato, chicken, fish |
| **B7** biotin | (biotin) | **carboxylases**: pyruvate → oxaloacetate (gluconeogenesis), fatty acid synthesis | dermatitis, **hair loss** (raw egg **avidin**) | liver, eggs, **gut bacteria** |
| **B9** folate | **THF** (blood: 5-methyl-THF) | **single-carbon transfers**: DNA (dUMP → dTMP), homocysteine → methionine (with B12) | **megaloblastic anaemia**; **neural tube defects** | chickpeas, liver, leafy greens, fortified |
